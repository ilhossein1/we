## University of Waterloo - Systems Design Engineering (gap fill only)

Checked 2026-10-07.

### Is a supervisor required before an offer (MASc)?
- **Yes.** Quote: "Finding a supervisor is mandatory in order to receive an offer of admission to the MASc program."
  URL: https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/faq
- On the same page, the MEng answer is the opposite: "Finding a supervisor is not mandatory in order to receive an offer of admission to the MEng program."
- How the department says to find a supervisor (same URL): "The Department of Systems Design Engineering recommends you apply, upload all of the required documents, and then start contacting potential supervisors." and "Once you have applied and all of your documents (including references) are uploaded, approaching a potential supervisor using the Faculty of Engineering's Contact a Faculty Member web form is a good next step and is the most effective way of initiating contact with a potential supervisor."
- Also on the same page: "Unfortunately, we are unable to add names of potential supervisors to your application once it has been submitted."

### Is there guaranteed funding for the MASc?
- **Yes ($18,000 per year for 2 years, full-time only).** Quote: "Guaranteed funding for incoming full-time MASc students of $18,000 per year for 2 years, with other funding opportunities like the EEF-M valued at up to $55,000 for over two years."
  URL: https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/faq
- Faculty of Engineering page: "All full-time students in the MASc and PhD programs are guaranteed a minimum level of funding while they are within their program time limits and in good academic standing." Table row: "MASc 6 terms (2 years) $18,000*". The footnote reads: "*If you are assessed with international tuition, visit international student funding." Also: "Part-time MASc and PhD students are not eligible for minimum funding."
  URL: https://uwaterloo.ca/engineering/graduate-students/funding-scholarships-and-awards/minimum-funding-and-other-support
- International add-on (not guaranteed for master's): "International Masters' Awards Of Excellence (IMAE) Valued at $2,500 / term for max. 5 terms. The IMAE will be awarded to a small number of selected international research master's students who meet the eligibility criteria."
  URL: https://uwaterloo.ca/engineering/graduate-students/funding-scholarships-and-awards/international-students-funding
- Caution, from the SYDE FAQ: "Students are expected to have their own personal funds to cover expenses while attending university." Also: "Every faculty member has limitations with respect to funding, space and time commitments". One SYDE professor, Thomas Willett, writes on his profile: "I am available to supervise research graduate students; however, I currently do not have funding." (https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/profile/twillett)
